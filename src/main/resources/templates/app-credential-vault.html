Credential Vault

Replaces the external spreadsheet of application passwords. Every password is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a data key that is itself wrapped by a key derived from your Master Password (Argon2id). Neither the Master Password nor any key is ever written to the database, a file, a log or the browser, and passwords stay masked on screen until you explicitly reveal or copy one after verifying the Master Password again. The vault locks itself after 10 minutes of inactivity.

Create Vault

Choose the Master Password that protects every stored credential. It is the root of trust: it cannot be recovered, and without it the vault cannot be opened.

At least 8 characters.

Unlock Vault

The vault is locked, so no key is in memory. Enter the Master Password to decrypt the data key for this session.
