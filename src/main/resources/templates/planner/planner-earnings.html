Monthly Planner — Earnings

Salary is normally entered once and repeated every month - tick Repeat and it carries into the following months on its own. Every line has a From date and, when it repeats, an Until month: the repeat starts on that date, so a yearly entry dated 29 January is not counted in the months before January, and it stops after its Until month. Both dates can be changed later without retyping the amount. A weekly entry counts the weeks that actually fall in the month (5 Mondays means 5 times the weekly figure). One time is a single dated amount - a bonus, a refund, a sale - that is planned in the month of its own date and never repeated. Freelance payouts and side-hustle incomes are typed in the month they are earned, and anything no listed type covers goes under Miscellaneous; click its (Note) link to describe the source, or use Add Miscellaneous for another Miscellaneous amount with its own note. A month you do not enter stays at zero.
