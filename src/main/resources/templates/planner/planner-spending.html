Monthly Planner — Spending (Obligations)

Credit card bills, fuel, shares, bonds, PPF, EPF/NPS and smallcase are entered each month, and anything no listed type covers goes under Miscellaneous; click its (Note) link to describe the source, or use Add Miscellaneous for another Miscellaneous obligation with its own note. Home Loan EMI is a regular monthly entry: tick Repeat and set the Until month of the last EMI. Every line has a From date, and One time is a single dated amount that is planned in its own month only. A weekly line counts the weeks that fall in the month, so a month with 5 Mondays costs 5 times the weekly figure. Bond SIP and Mutual Fund SIP are filled in automatically from their own screens. Credit Card Bills comes from the transactions you save on the Credit Card Bills Entry screen: each transaction is billed in the month of its billing cycle end plus the card's grace period, and this month's line is the sum of the transactions billed in the month selected above.
