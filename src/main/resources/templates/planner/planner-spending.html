Monthly Planner — Spending (Obligations)

Credit card bills, fuel, shares, bonds, PPF, EPF/NPS and smallcase are entered each month, and anything no listed type covers goes under Miscellaneous; click its (Note) link to describe the source, or use Add Miscellaneous for another Miscellaneous obligation with its own note. Home Loan EMI is a regular monthly entry: tick Repeat and set the Until month of the last EMI. Every line has a From date, and One time is a single dated amount that is planned in its own month only. A weekly line counts the weeks that fall in the month, so a month with 5 Mondays costs 5 times the weekly figure. Bond SIP and Mutual Fund SIP are filled in automatically from their own screens. Credit Card Bills comes from the cards you save with Credit Cards: each card's expense is planned in the month its billing cycle ends.
