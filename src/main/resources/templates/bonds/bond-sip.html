Bond SIP

Set up a systematic bond investment (BONDBP_V7/V9). Each due instalment becomes a bond record on the Bond Records screen - status In Progress, Investment Type SIP Investment - where you complete the remaining details, which turns it EXECUTED. A past SIP date is backfilled with every instalment already due, and a nightly job (00:00), the daily execution (00:01) and every application restart keep the records in sync.

How it works

  • The Issuer drop-down lists the issuers configured in Application Parameter Configuration (param name: issuer), refreshed from the App Config object every 15 minutes. SIP Date, SIP Amount and Frequency are mandatory; Investment Date is stamped to today (BONDBP_V9 #3).
  • Status starts as CREATED and is never editable (#3); it turns INPROGRESS once the first instalment is generated.
  • On each due SIP date one bond record is created on the Bond Records screen: Issuer and Investment Date carried over, SIP Date as Interest Payment Start Date, SIP Amount as Investment Amount per Unit, Frequency as Coupon Frequency - everything else blank for you to complete (#4.b-#4.f). Weekend dates roll to the next weekday.
  • Saving the completed details on the Bond Details Entry screen turns the record from In Progress to EXECUTED (#4.h). The banner above confirms when every due instalment is present.
