Bond SIP Transactions

One row per instalment, auto-created per the Bond SIP's frequency schedule (BONDBP_V7 #4) - a SIP entered with a past date is backfilled with every instalment due so far, all INPROGRESS here. Issuer, Create Date, SIP Date (that instalment's due date, weekend dates rolled to the next weekday) and SIP Amount are fixed by the SIP and cannot be changed (#5). Enter the Actual SIP Amount and associate a Bond Name from the drop-down (your own and your family members' bonds, shown as Bond Name|PAN) - that transaction then turns EXECUTED (#6/#7).
