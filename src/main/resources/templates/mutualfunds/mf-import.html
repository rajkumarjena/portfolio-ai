Upload CAS statement

Supported: MF Central detailed report (xlsx), CAS statement (pdf) and the csv export of the same columns.

What happens next

  1. Rows are parsed and checked against the report totals.
  2. You review the reconciliation report - green means totals match.
  3. Duplicates are skipped automatically (MF.md 22).
  4. New AMCs/schemes can be remapped or registered on accept (MF.md 20).
  5. Only Accept writes the rows to your portfolio (MF.md 16).
